01

What is included

Project aggregate counts, review-state summaries, and market-series coverage metadata. The event target covers Iranian state armed forces acting abroad, or foreign state armed forces attacking Iran or Iranian state military assets/personnel abroad.

02

What is still unknown

Historical event coverage, defensible negative windows, precise information availability and some source rights remain unresolved. Unreviewed records remain. No probabilities or trading signals are published.

03

How weekly updates work

A maintainer reviews the evidence and refreshes the approved summary, then manually publishes the page. There is no automatic weekly refresh. The review date and market sample date are shown separately.

04

Sources and attribution

Market metadata comes from saved FRED, Yahoo Finance and attempted TSETMC acquisitions. Review-queue summaries derive from UCDP GED 26.1 and Candidate 2026 Q3 (Uppsala Conflict Data Program, CC BY 4.0). Inclusion counts come from project curation, not an official UCDP conflict statistic.

Underlying price data and full source documents are not redistributed here. This page does not establish rights to train on or republish those histories.
